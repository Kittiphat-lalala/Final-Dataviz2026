## 3. Proposed dashboard structure

### Tab Q1 — Thailand–ASEAN comparison with separate context

**Main comparison:** horizontal country bars of `limits_on_content`, same selected `data_year`, Thailand highlighted. Unit: **points /35; higher = greater content freedom**. Tooltips show `country`, `iso3`, `data_year`, `report_edition`; do not substitute `total_score` for this component.

- **KPIs:** Thailand component = the THA row's `limits_on_content` (points /35); selected-peer mean = sum(peer component scores)/number of available selected peer countries in that year (points /35, unweighted, excluding Thailand); Thailand-minus-peer-mean (points); coverage = available selected peers / requested selected peers (countries). Empty peer selection produces unavailable comparison KPIs.
- **Thai context:** a weekly table of `week_start`, `week_end`, `acsc_highest_loss_type_th`, `acsc_flag`, `acsc_loss_precision`, `acsc_source_url`. Caption: “Type reported as having the highest loss in this reporting period.” Missing names stay unavailable. This is categorical briefing information, not a measured total loss by that type. Optional numeric type-loss detail is gated below.
- **Global AI context:** horizontal distribution bars = distinct `Incident ID` per `Risk Domain` in independently selected occurrence years (incidents). Include Unclassified and show classified IDs / all IDs ×100 (%). Optional percentage uses **all filtered incident IDs** as denominator, with its definition visible. No Thailand/country attribution is imposed on this global panel.
- **Periods:** Freedom `data_year` 2010–2024 (edition = year + 1); Thai reported periods 2025-11-09–2026-10-24; AI occurrence dates 1983-09-26–2026-10-22 in the 2026-10-26 export. Display the active range separately above each panel. Context is not a same-period comparison with Freedom.
- **Filters/defaults and Reset:** Freedom year 2024; Thailand fixed plus all seven available peer candidates selected, pending final team selection. Thai context defaults to the available briefing range, showing all records and warnings. AI context defaults to all occurrence years/all domains. Each control changes only its panel. Reset restores these settings.
- **In-tab quality:** list selected peers absent in the chosen year; show source-specific coverage and four Thai flags. No joined risk index, correlation, causal conclusion or extra international dataset.

### Tab Q2 — Occurrence-year AI incidents and deepfake subset

- **Chart:** yearly lines by `Risk Domain`, with a separate Unclassified series; y = distinct `Incident ID` in each (`year`, domain) group (incidents), x = occurrence `year`. A separate aligned chart shows total distinct incidents and the candidate deepfake subset. Deepfake is an overlapping subset, not an eighth Risk Domain or an additive stacked category.
- **KPIs:** all recorded incidents = distinct IDs; classified incidents = distinct IDs with nonblank domain; classification coverage = classified/all ×100 (%); candidate deepfake incidents = distinct matching IDs (incidents); candidate share = matching/all ×100 (%). Coverage is computed after the year filter **before** the domain filter; display both selected-domain counts and the full year-filtered denominator so selecting classified domains cannot hide missing classification. Show yearly coverage too.
- **Proposed keyword rule v1, not finalized:** search each of `Implicated Systems`, `title`, `description` separately, case-insensitively, after Unicode NFKC normalization and converting Unicode dash characters to a plain hyphen. Match the whole-token pattern `\bdeep[\s-]?fakes?\b` in any field; this covers deepfake/deepfakes, deep fake(s) and deep-fake(s). Missing text is skipped, never treated as evidence of absence. Record matched field, term, excerpt, Incident ID and rule version. Deduplicate IDs across matched fields. A nonmatch means “no keyword match,” not “not a deepfake.” Do not equate `Risk Domain=Misinformation` with deepfake.
- **Actual example review:** the candidate case-insensitive token pattern was run on the uploaded fields before the proposed Unicode-normalization step. ID **39**, “Deepfake Obama Introduction of Deepfakes,” matches title/description. ID **1112**, “Scammer Reportedly Used AI Voice Clone of WCPO Cincinnati Meteorologist in Facebook Fraud Attempts,” matches `Implicated Systems`, illustrating why title-only matching misses examples. ID **1785**, “Spain's People's Party Reportedly Published Purported Deepfakes Featuring Fabricated Admissions of Misconduct,” matches systems/title. ID **728**, “AI Firm Lovo Reportedly Accused of Illegally Replicating Voice Actors' Voices,” does **not** match that pattern despite voice-cloning/synthetic-audio system labels: it is a boundary example for review, not an asserted false negative. These are source text observations, not externally verified event judgments.
- **Review gate:** examine full source descriptions and matched excerpts for these examples plus varied matches/nonmatches. Decide whether explicit keyword mentions alone define the subset or whether voice cloning/synthetic media terms and contextual exclusions belong in it. Assess negation, incidental mentions and potential missed cases. Do not report accuracy or finalize a deepfake count before the normalized rule is rerun and the team approves the definition. “Purported” source wording must remain visible.
- **Filters/defaults and Reset:** full occurrence-year range; all domains including Unclassified; deepfake comparison visible with “provisional rule” label until approved. Domain filtering changes counts in both charts consistently, but the coverage strip retains the year-filtered base described above. Reset restores these defaults. No country filter, entity analysis or report-count metric is needed for this question.
- **In-tab quality:** source snapshot, classified/unclassified counts, estimated-date unknowns and keyword-rule limitations. Empty years inside the source range may show zero recorded incidents only after confirming source completeness for the selected snapshot; dates outside its coverage are unavailable.

- **Charts:** three vertically aligned weekly plots, each with its own labelled y-axis; gaps for missing/excluded weeks. Display weekly KPI cards for the latest eligible week within the selected range, with its date and any newer missing/excluded week disclosed. A range summary, if shown, sums cases and losses over the **same eligible periods**, and computes sum(loss)/sum(cases); never sum weekly averages or average them without weighting. Show included/reported/selected period counts.
- **Annotations/table:** `acsc_top_type_th` means most frequent type; `acsc_highest_loss_type_th` means highest-loss type. They may differ. Retain 12–18 April and 23–29 August totals with warnings; do not use their published changes as recomputed differences. Whole-week selection only. Reset restores these settings and clears selected points. The 25–31 October row remains visible as unavailable, not silently dropped.

## 5. Interpretation rules

- Scores are expert assessments; incidents are curated recorded events; AFNC counts are published posts/verdicts; ACSC counts are reported police cases collected through briefing articles. None is a count of all underlying digital harm.
- Higher Freedom scores mean greater freedom, including the component scores. Scores and classifications must not be summed with counts or money into an unapproved risk index.
- Distinguish no row, missing field, unclassified, excluded-quality row and observed zero. A country with no assigned AI incidents is not demonstrated safe. A non-verdict post is not a True verdict.
- Preserve source selection effects: changing country coverage, AIID news/language and taxonomy coverage, sparse early AFNC publishing, police reporting and briefing availability. Category totals after multi-label expansion may exceed unique incidents/posts.
- Similar movements in fact-checking publications and reported cases do not establish causation, prevalence or effectiveness. Do not infer a lag, causal relationship or cross-country risk ranking from these sources alone.
- Attribute AIID to Responsible AI Collaborative; Freedom scores to Freedom House via QoG Standard Dataset (January 2026), with instructor transformations; AFNC to its official export; ACSC to compiled briefings with each row's publisher, article date and URL. These attributions come from supplied materials; no external content was retrieved. Do not imply full-text availability or an unstated reuse licence.
